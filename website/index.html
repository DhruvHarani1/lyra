A look is not a theme colour. It changes the type, the way each word arrives, the background, the player card, the cover frame, the Settings panel and even the card you share. Pick a favourite, cycle with one key, or let Lyra choose by the song.

02 · Every state, designed

No lyrics? Still beautiful.

Most players show an empty box when something is missing. In Lyra each look has its own screen for synced words, words without timing, no lyrics found, and a video playing behind. Scroll through the four states of one look.

STATE 01

Synced, with the video

Every word lights up as it is sung, over the official video, which pauses and resumes with the music.

STATE 02

Words without timing

Some songs only have plain lyrics. They are shown as a calm, static page that never pretends to follow the song, and Lyra keeps looking for a synced version.

STATE 03

No lyrics found

A designed screen instead of an error: the reason in plain words, a live retry countdown, and ways out: search again, pick a match, paste your own.

STATE 04

No video available

The look carries the scene on its own, so a missing video never leaves a hole.

03 · Made to share

A card for every look.

Tap a lyric to make a story-size card. It wears the look you are in: hand-drawn doodle marks on a stencil, a terminal printout, a sticker, a diary page, a scoreboard. Everything is drawn on your device.

04 · Down to the details

Even Settings
wears the look.

The cover gets an animated frame (a taped polaroid, a neon tube, a film edge), and the Settings panel is re-skinned in paper, border, type and pen colour. Nothing to configure: the look is designed, so you do not have to.

10 · Get it

Get Lyra.

Free forever. Pick your device.

Windows

Windows 10 / 11

Download installer
  1. Install the Spotify desktop app and play a song.
  2. Run the installer. Lyra opens as a small floating widget.
  3. Click it to expand, press F for fullscreen, V for the next look.

If Windows SmartScreen warns, choose More info → Run anyway. The app is open source and not yet code-signed.

Android

Android 8.0 or newer

Download APK
  1. Open the APK and allow installs from your browser or files app.
  2. Grant Notification access so Lyra can see what is playing.
  3. Play a song. Pick a look from the Look tab in Settings.

Google Play Protect may block installs made straight from a browser. If you see App blocked to protect your device: open the Play Store, tap your picture → Play Protect → the gear → switch off Scan apps with Play Protect, install Lyra, then switch it back on. Or use adb install, or Obtainium. Lyra asks for Notification access to see what is playing, which is why Google is careful; the code is open on GitHub.
