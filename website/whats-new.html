0.26

Windows 0.26 · Android 0.12
  • Song changes have an effect. When the song changes, the screen cross-animates from the old song to the new one, and every change uses a different effect: fade, dissolve, slide, rise, zoom, iris, wipe or split. It never repeats the same one twice in a row. It is on by default and can be switched off in Look. It stays off when Reduce motion is on, in Light performance mode, and in a WebView that does not support it.
  • Volume that eases between songs. Optional (off by default): the volume eases down over the last second and a half of a song and back up over the first second and a half of the next. It only works where Lyra can set your volume (the Windows system volume, or a Spotify device that allows it), it hands your volume back if you pause, skip back or close Lyra, and it stands down the moment you move the volume yourself.

0.25

Windows 0.25 · Android 0.11
  • Wallet shows its whole screen again. Its card stack and video window had been hidden by a style clash with Drift Wall.
  • Lyra Connect is safer. The host only answers requests addressed to your PC's own network address, device tokens are encrypted on disk where Windows allows it, and a device that disconnects now leaves the list straight away.
  • Android TV can pair with your PC. Type the 8-character code from the Devices panel, or pick your computer from the list. It has not been tried on a real TV yet.
  • Lighter edges. The animated screen edge no longer works when it is hidden, paused or switched off, so it uses less battery.
  • Easier to read on small windows. Plain, unsynced lyrics and the small notice above them keep a readable size on phones and in the mini player. Artwork lyrics sit on a darker backdrop.
  • Smarter look picks, steadier updates. The smart pick no longer matches words inside other words, and the releases are now only built from a commit whose checks passed.

0.24.1

Windows 0.24.1 · Android 0.10.1
  • A mini player with a look of its own. Twelve looks now arrange the small window differently: a cinema screen with curtains, a sunset panorama, a framed print, a taped polaroid, a viewfinder, a street-view inset, a code preview pane, a dewdrop, a pasted poster, a hero frame and more. Picto’s icon frieze and Artwork’s lyric size were adjusted to fit.
  • The player card wears the look in ten more looks. Stencil, Film Reel, Film Strip, Lock Screen, Wallet, Web, Lens, Picto, Drift Wall and Split-flap now dress the mini player card and the phone dock in their own colours, borders and type.
  • Android, look by look. Every look now has its own player dock, play button and progress bar on the phone, the video status pill no longer covers the header, light looks no longer get a grey band behind the lyrics, and the edge effect stays off the navigation row. The home-screen widget has its own colours for every look.
  • Fixes. Lyrics that clipped in the mini window and on phones (DM, Terminal, Stadium, Dream, Stencil, Editor).

0.24

Windows 0.24 · Android 0.10
  • 34 looks, all different. Three new looks, Journal (a ruled diary page with a taped polaroid), Doodle (fat marker on dot-grid paper) and Dusk Ridge (a star-filled sky over sunlit mountains), and every look was redesigned in full screen so none shares a layout with another.
  • Every look has its own edge. The window edge now moves with the song: a neon tube that fills clockwise, a scribbled border that redraws itself, LED chase lights, flipping departure-board tiles, a GPS dot, silk threads that twang, and more, each driven by the line being sung.
  • Editor shows real code. Lyrics become real, syntax-highlighted source in Java, Kotlin, TypeScript, Python, Rust, Go or C#, chosen by the song, with line numbers, a git gutter, a minimap and a status bar.
  • Journal marks lyrics like a pen would. Black underlines, blue ballpoint circles, yellow, pink and green highlighters, red strike-throughs with a rewritten word, margin stars and a bracket for the chorus.
  • DM is a real chat. Lines arrive as voice notes, polls, locations, stickers, contact cards, photos, link previews, replies and reactions, with read ticks and a typing header.
  • Fixes. Lyrics that clipped or overlapped on small windows and tablets, unreadable text in a few looks with unsynced lyrics, and leftover rules that dimmed Cinema and others.

0.23

Windows 0.23 · Android 0.9
  • 31 looks, each its own screen. Every look now has its own video window, lyric style, player card and edge effect, in every state (video, no video, synced, plain, nothing playing). Eleven new looks: Film Strip, Lock Screen, Wallet, Route, Editor, Metro, Web, Lens, Picto, Drift Wall and Split-flap.
  • The cover wears the look. Doodles and a picture treatment per look on the player card, and on the share card too.
  • Lyra Connect. Pair devices with a QR code and share the song clock, lyrics, video, look and your choices. Remote control, follow-me and party mode.
  • Smarter picks. Synced lyrics always rank above plain ones, videos are ranked better, your manual picks are remembered per song, and everything is cached so a song you played before starts instantly.
  • Videos join in time when they load late, YouTube captions are off, and videos that are not 16:9 show whole over a blurred fill.
  • A simpler Settings page that wears the look, with search.
  • Karaoke and recording: a count-in, word-by-word fill and smarter version picks; recording picks a preset for your PC and uses less memory.
  • Android TV. The same APK now has a TV screen: big lyrics, D-pad and remote control, a low-power screen saver. Pairing with your PC is not wired up yet, so on a TV it follows the TV's own media session.
  • OBS overlay. A transparent lyrics page for streamers, served by Lyra Connect with its own view-only link you can revoke.
  • Android: all looks on phone and tablet, eight widgets with their own looks, a better notification, a lock-screen card and an always-on lyrics screen, car mode for passengers, and an in-app updater.

0.22

Windows 0.22 · Android 0.8
  • A card for every look. The player card, the mini player and the phone dock each wear the look, in all 20.
  • Stencil and Film Reel. Two more motion-graphics looks: spray-paint words on concrete, and sepia film with light leaks.
  • Android widget in your look’s colours, and an Android queue so the next song can be prepared in advance where the player shares one.
  • Optional start gate (off by default): hold a song for at most a second and a half while lyrics and video are still loading.
  • An automated check that the video pauses and resumes with the music within 300 ms.

0.21

Windows 0.21 · Android 0.7
  • Sketchbook, Glitch and Pop Comic. Motion-graphics looks built on a new lyric style: one giant word at a time thrown at the camera.
  • Every look has its own screens for words without timing, no lyrics found, and a video playing behind.
  • Next-song preload on Windows with Spotify: lyrics, video and the Apple preview clip are ready before the song starts.
  • The phone’s YouTube video now pauses with the music.
  • Fixes: Terminal panes no longer overlap when a video plays; Diary is readable over dark video; covers keep thin frames in small docks.

0.20

Windows 0.20 · Android 0.6
  • The look is designed, not configured. Layout, typeface, size, spacing and animation controls are gone from Settings; each look decides.
  • A share card, a Settings panel and an animated cover frame for every look.
  • Apple preview clips cached on disk so they stop stuttering; videos that failed once are remembered for that song.
  • Synced lyrics always win over plain ones, and plain ones are re-checked for a synced version.

0.19

Windows 0.19 · Android 0.5
  • Per-look layouts: dock, floating pill, subtitle bar, chat column, terminal panes.
  • Android tablet and foldable two-pane layout, and a live home-screen widget.
  • Simpler Settings in six plain tabs, and automated screenshot tests of every look on every size.

0.18

Windows 0.18 · Android 0.4
  • Vibes arrive: Neon, Chrome, Dream, Terminal, Stickers, DM, Diary, Arcade, Cinema, Sunset Drive, Blocks, Stadium, Cassette and Trailer.
  • Cycle looks with V, or let Lyra choose by the song. A resilient lyrics and video engine with retries and a memory of your choices.
